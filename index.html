How we build

  1. We start from real operations

    Planvio did not begin on a whiteboard. It began as an internal system built to run an actual services business, with real crews, real invoices, and real month-end pain. Then we rebuilt it as a product.

  2. One system of record, always

    Most business software creates reconciliation work: two tools hold the same number and disagree. We build so that cannot happen, because there is only ever one number.

  3. Your data stays in your environment

    Our software deploys into infrastructure you already own and control, under your existing security and compliance settings. We do not keep a copy of your business.

  4. You talk to the people who build it

    We are a small team by choice. When something needs to change, it does not sit in a queue behind a thousand other customers.

About Halvyn

Halvyn is a software company building operations platforms for project-based businesses. Not task lists and not accounting packages, but the layer in between that most companies end up running on spreadsheets: what the job was quoted at, what it is costing, who is working on it, and whether it is making money.

That problem looks the same whether the work is a software rollout or a building fit-out, which is why our software is not built for one industry. The names on the screens change. The shape of the problem does not.

We are early, and we would rather say so than pretend otherwise. If you are evaluating us, you will be talking to the people writing the code.
